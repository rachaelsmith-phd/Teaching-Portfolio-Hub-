# Criminological Theory Simulation

## Overview

This activity transforms criminological theory from content to be memorized into an experience to be explored.

Students follow an interactive case and evaluate behavior through multiple theoretical perspectives.

---

## Theories Included

- Classical Theory
- Strain Theory
- Social Learning Theory
- Control Theory
- Labeling Theory
- Routine Activities Theory

---

## Learning Goals

Students learn:

- Theory influences interpretation.
- Multiple explanations may exist for a single event.
- Professional decisions are shaped by theoretical assumptions.

---

## Technology Used

- Canvas Modules
- Conditional Navigation
- Embedded Media
- Reflection Activities

---

# Professional Identity Assignment

## Overview

This assignment encourages students to reflect on who they are becoming as professionals.

---

## Guiding Questions

- Why did I enter this profession?
- What values guide my work?
- What responsibilities accompany professional authority?
- What kind of professional do I hope to become?

---

## Learning Outcomes

Students will:

- Develop self-awareness
- Examine professional values
- Explore leadership identity
- Connect coursework with career goals

---

## Connection to Research

This assignment aligns closely with my research on educator identity, professional identity development, and transformative learning.
